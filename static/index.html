How AgriFlow Works

A simple, transparent, and location-grounded verification workflow connecting local agriculture to open markets.

1

Farmer Submission

Farmers record their active cultivation details, land size, expected yield, and harvest dates. Submissions are tagged with their specific village and area.

2

Officer Verification

Local Agriculture Officers review incoming farmer submissions in their assigned jurisdiction. Officers directly inspect, verify, or reject submissions with feedback.

3

Public Discovery & Contact

Buyers easily discover verified produce by crop, district, and area. They can review quality grades and send controlled purchase inquiries without complex logins.

For Farmers

  • Submit upcoming harvest yields for local verification
  • Track real-time status: Pending → Verified / Rejected

Jurisdiction Control & Trust
